LIVING CELESTIAL OBSERVATORY: the interface should feel like an intimate artificial world observed through a quiet instrument. Dark-only. Generous cinematic pacing for the world view, tighter pacing for controls. Deep ink surfaces, glass-black panels, fine luminous rules, one restrained lunar-gold accent, and small cool-white telemetry details. Display typography is an elegant editorial serif; utility text is compact and neutral. Motion should feel physical and atmospheric rather than flashy: slow drift, soft parallax, restrained glow, and deliberate transitions. Nothing should resemble a generic AI dashboard.
